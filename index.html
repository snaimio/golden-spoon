The Story

A Heritage of Passion & Mastery

Golden Spoon began with a singular vision: to remove culinary pretension while elevating flavor, texture, and visual storytelling to its highest possible aesthetic form.

Under the leadership of Executive Chef Anthony Mercer, our kitchen works in close partnership with organic heritage farmers, coastal fishermen, and artisan cheese makers to ensure every ingredient arrives at peak flavor.
